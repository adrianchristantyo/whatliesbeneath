The year is 305 A.F. [[Booby]] moved to Axenfell from Bree a few years ago in search of arcane knowledge. [[Æget]] also grew up there to Norlandic parents. Living his life as a scribe, it was easy for Æget to say the least. Booby, on the other hand, worked and studied hard almost every day, spending most of his time at the libraries spread across Axenfell, but mainly in [[Bryndemere]], the capital city of Axenfell. During his time at the libraries, he would eavesdrop on many conversations, either intentionally or purely by accident. One he could never shake was the talks about a potential dethroning of the current royal family by House Rocke with help from some "force from the East". He couldn't believe it. The East, for all Booby knew, couldn't be anything else other than the Green Lands and the Suncloaks, who were slowly yet surely increasing in power. For all Booby knew, the Suncloaks forbade arcane and use of magic. A threat to Booby's pursuit of arcane knowledge.

This feeling of panic and anxiety lingered for a couple more days before finally, Booby decided to take matters into his own hands. He had to move. Fast. He took whatever books he could take. But he had one more thing that he wanted, a book that he overheard about that contained valuable arcane information. However, he was no thief. He barely had the instinct to hide from enemies. Strolling down the streets, he tried to find a way to get that book. With his eyes glued to the floor and his head spiraling for ideas, he bumped into [[Rok]].
