Status: **DEAD** 
One of the wolves from [[Children of the Truth]] 

The [[Followers of Bjørn]] met him in [[Mossmere]] attracting him and two more wolves with the illusion of a cow created by [[Booby]] and [[Æget]]. 
[[Booby]] made him shine and after a fight he ran away so the [[Followers of Bjørn]] chased him, leading them to [[Lyra Farrona]].

Later on, in [[Whitmore]], [[Æget]] stumbled on him, started a fight with him and his group which ended up on his death.

Now his corpse is being transported in the [[Bag of Holding]] of [[Una]].
