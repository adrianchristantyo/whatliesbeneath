[[Bonn]] and [[Æget]] are in [[The Tiny Knife]] as usual when they see [[Rok]] going in, shocked by the amount of people in the tavern. [[Bonn]] notices it and pulls her to the table.
[[Booby]] goes into [[The Tiny Knife]] and recognizes [[Rok]], as he hasn't seen her in so long, he decides to join to the table.
Finally [[Bonn]] notices [[Sister M]] and pulls her to the table.

All of the members of the party get into a conversation where they meet each other without being aware of how this night is about to change their lifes.

This conversation gets interrupted when [[Loznoth Bronzemane]] comes to [[Æget]] asking for help with monsters in his hometown, [[Mossmere]]. [[Æget]] accepts to help him asking for 65 [[gold coins]] in return, a couple of rounds for the people in the table and some basic equipment.

When the night ends, the party gets sepparated. [[Booby]] and [[Rok]] go into the woods to meditate, [[Sister M]] goes to the church and [[Bonn]] and [[Æget]] stay in the tavern for a while.

The party meets again in [[The Tiny Knife]] the next morning. [[Bonn]] and [[Sister M]] arrive earlier than the rest. 

Going into [[The Tiny Knife]], [[Rok]] notices [[Aiden]] out of the tavern, drunk dead. [[Sister M]] and [[Rok]] go to him and [[Booby]] recognizes him as a distant member of his family, so [[Rok]] gives him a hangover cure. After this the entire group goes into the tavern.

Once everyone is there, [[Loznoth Bronzemane]] prepares breakfast for all. [[Booby]] tries to charm [[Loznoth Bronzemane]] to get more breakfast, but he can't [[Loznoth Bronzemane]] notices it.

[[Loznoth Bronzemane]] tells the group that wolves are attacking the farms of [[Mossmere]] and asks the group to look for the village chief to know what is happening. 

The group heads to [[Mossmere]], stopping halfway there to camp. [[Bonn]] is attacked by a bear, the party fights it, kills it and cooks it to eat during the night. [[Rok]] takes [[Bear fur]] from the dead bear.
