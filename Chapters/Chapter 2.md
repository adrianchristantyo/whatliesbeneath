The party finally reaches [[Mossmere]]. A small village with a small number of citizens, between 50 and 70.

They go into the tavern of the town, there is no much people there. [[Rok]] sees a person with a monocle in the most crowded area, he is the chief of the village [[Erwin Nørgaard]].

[[Booby]] and [[Æget]] go to [[Erwin Nørgaard]], [[Booby]] starts asking questions while from the distance [[Æget]] detects thoughts on him.

[[Erwin Nørgaard]] seems to not know anything about wolves in the town, but then a thought comes to his mind, all he knows is that they attack during the night, freezes mid sentence and tells [[Booby]] to ask the local farmers. [[Æget]] detects a thought in [[Erwin Nørgaard]] mind saying "They shouldn't know". Then the bartender offers [[Booby]] and [[Æget]] a pint which they take.

[[Rok]], [[Bonn]] and [[Sister M]]  go to a table of 5 ladies, and asks them about the wolves. One lady, [[Amanda]] tells them there is no livestock left and tells them to go to [['Sir' Davis]]' house, he might have information as he saw some of the wolves leaving after an attack.

[[Sister M]] evangelized [[Amanda]], getting 5 [[gold coins]] and for [[Amanda]] to guide her [[Rok]] and [[Bonn]] to [['Sir' Davis]] house.

[[Booby]] talks with the right hand of [[Erwin Nørgaard]], [[Anders Andersen]]. [[Booby]] trauma dumps about his veganism and then asks about the situation. [[Anders Andersen]] tell him to "stop the wolves". He also says that no humans have been killed, most of them were injured and just one hardly injured.

As there is no cattle, the main kingdom is helping [[Mossmere]] meanwhile. There are supplies on the way, and there is good harvest. 

[[Booby]] asks about the severe injured person, [['Sir' Davis]] and gets the instructions to get to his house. He also asks to get some equipment for fighting the wolves but there is no supply in the town.

On the way to [['Sir' Davis]]'s house, the party notices the town going more and more quiet.

[[Rok]], [[Bonn]] and [[Sister M]] arrived to [['Sir' Davis]]'s house first and knocked at the door three times. They hear a lot of rumbling and when the door opens, they see a farmer in his 30-40s in a very bad condition. [[Rok]] comments she wants to kill all the farmers, which weirds him out and as he tries to close the door, he is stopped by [[Bonn]]. [[Rok]] tries to convince him of talking by offering the [[Bear fur]], but he rejects it.

At thaat moment [[Booby]] and [[Æget]] get to the house. [[Booby]] charms [['Sir' Davis]] to let the party go into the house. He recieves the party and give them bread.

[['Sir' Davis]] tells the party what happened, showing them the scar of his fight with the wolves. The party goes outside to see what happened, seeing the injured stock, the surviving cattle and a trail of blood. [[Bonn]] tries to sense animals around to see what happened but there were no animals nearby.

The party leave into the woods, [['Sir' Davis]] realized he was charmed by [[Booby]]. 

The team finds a clearing in the woods, [[Booby]] and [[Æget]] make a minor illusion to create a cow and use [[Booby]]'s blood to bait the wolves. The party hides very poorly and get so bored that they start singing.

Everybody see 3 wolves coming by, stepping closer and closer to the cow. The one closest to it takes a look at it and leap into the cow, passing through it and falling. The wolves notice the party and a fight starts.

[[Æget]] influenced by [[Booby]] not wanting to kill the wolves, tries to speak with them. They don't pay attention and attack at him, making him fall unconcious, not before asking "Who sends you?" and getting answered "she did".

During the fight, when one of the wolves is about to faint, he rises up instead, not as a wolf but as a human. Thus, the party baptized this character as [['Jacob']]. [['Jacob']] and the two wolves flee from the fight, the members of the party decide to go after him, ending in a path full of wolves.

The alpha wolf transforms into a human, looks at the group and tell them "leave now, and you will have peace". She answers a purpose the group don't understand.

[[Sister M]] asked her to not bother the village, she agrees saying: "Leave now and I won't bother the village". The name of the alpha wolf is [[Lyra]].

While leaving, the party percieves a lot of wolves ready to jump at them, a track of blood and something being dragged to the cave. They also see an owl.

The party go back to [[Mossmere]] and asks [['Sir' Davis]] to stay in his shack to rest, he lets them spend the night in the guest room.
