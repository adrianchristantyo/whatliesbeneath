Full name: Rokohnach Vugarith Bokuxach
Status: **ALIVE**
As a kid, she grew up poor and lived life by stealing from others. It didn't help that she also suffered from schizophrenia. She was bullied from a young age.
She was a kid when she was paid by [[Booby]] to help him steal from [[Æget]] and take [[Æget's lost book]]. After fleeing from Axenfell to [[Bree]] with Booby, she decides to leave him and reconnect with nature.
She finds the [[Monastery of Torben]] that take her into their care and try to cure her schizophrenia. The results were unsatisfactory, and now her episodes are less extreme than it used to be. Rok comes back to Whitmore to reconnect with Booby and deal with her past as a way to pass her training as a monk at the Monastery of Torben.
