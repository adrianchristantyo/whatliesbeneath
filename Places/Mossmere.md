The village in [[Bree]] where the brother of [[Loznoth Bronzemane]], [[Peter Bronzemane]] lives. The [[Followers of Bjørn]] were sent there to deal with some problems related to wolves.

It is a small village, with comething in between 50 and 70 citizens.

The village chief is [[Erwin Nørgaard]], and his right hand man [[Anders Andersen]].

The village is suffering from constant attacks from wolves to the cattle. The main kingdom has been supplying food to them.
